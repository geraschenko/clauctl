compact-history-hidden decided: differ

claude's resumed scrollback drops every turn before the boundary and offers them behind ctrl+o; clauctl renders the whole file (the pre-compact prompt and answer stay visible above the boundary). Proposed differ: clauctl's transcript is the session file, the tree and context views already distinguish "in context" from "on disk", and hiding history would remove the only place a reader sees what the summary replaced. Revisit if the transcript grows a context-only mode.

compact-boundary-line decided: differ

claude's marker is · Conversation compacted (ctrl+o for history); clauctl's is context compacted (17k → 1.4k tokens) — the token counts are the point of the line (they come from compactMetadata, absent on claude's line), and the ctrl+o hint would be false under the entry above. Proposed differ; the collapsed Compacted (ctrl+o to see full summary) summary line beneath it is the already-decided compact-boundary entry (differ: full summary on expand).

compact-command-result decided: match (implemented)

claude now renders the command's stdout as a ⎿ Compacted result line under ❯ /compact. clauctl hides /compact stdout on purpose (transcript.test.ts "/compact stdout and 'No response requested.' are hidden"), matching the 2.1.211 capture where claude showed a bare ❯ /compact; that observation was of a no-op compact. Proposed match: drop the hide so /compact gets the same ⎿ result line as /login (slash-command entry, decided match). The "No response requested." suppression is unaffected — claude still hides that.

compact-block-spacing decided: match (implemented)

clauctl leaves two blank lines after the boundary line and two after the summary line (one between every other pair of blocks). Block spacing was decided match in the 2.1.211 catalog (exactly one blank line between blocks); the boundary and summary components each carry their own top/bottom padding on top of the turn spacing. Proposed match: one blank line, as around every other block.
